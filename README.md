# Growth Marketing Research Project

## Chosen Topic
LinkedIn organic content strategy for B2B SaaS.

## Research Question
What patterns do strong B2B SaaS voices use on LinkedIn to build trust, educate their audience, and support organic demand generation?

## Why I Chose This Topic
I chose LinkedIn organic content strategy because it is directly connected to digital marketing, content strategy, and organic growth. Since B2B SaaS is new to me, this topic helped me learn more about how SaaS founders and marketers use LinkedIn to build visibility and trust.

## Method
For this stage, I manually collected public LinkedIn posts from selected experts and organized them by author.

I did not use an API for this stage because this workflow is new to me. I wanted to focus first on understanding the topic, choosing relevant experts, collecting useful examples, and organizing the research clearly.

## Repository Structure

- `research/sources.md` — list of selected experts with links and short annotations
- `research/linkedin-posts/` — collected LinkedIn post examples organized by expert

## Experts Included
I selected 10 experts in total in `research/sources.md`.

For deeper content collection, I focused on 5 experts:
1. Dave Gerhardt
2. Tommy Clark
3. TK Kader
4. Mark Jung
5. Adam Robinson

## Initial Findings
Across the collected content, I noticed several patterns:

- Strong posts often start with a clear hook, such as a number, question, personal statement, or tension.
- B2B SaaS LinkedIn content often connects marketing ideas to business results, such as trust, pipeline, retention, positioning, and growth.
- Founder-led content feels more personal and human than traditional company content.
- Practical examples, AI tools, recognizable companies, and market insights make posts feel more credible.
- The best posts are not only educational; they also build authority and trust over time.

## Notes
This project is an early-stage research base. Since B2B SaaS and GitHub-based research workflows are new to me, I treated this as a learning project and focused on completing the task clearly, honestly, and in an organized way.
