# Research Sources — LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy (B2B SaaS)

## Topic Choice
LinkedIn organic content strategy for B2B SaaS companies.

## Why This Topic
LinkedIn is a primary channel for B2B SaaS growth, especially for founder-led and content-driven strategies. It allows direct access to decision-makers, making it a strong platform for organic demand generation.

## Goal
Identify high-signal experts and collect real examples of effective LinkedIn content to understand patterns, structure, and strategy.
